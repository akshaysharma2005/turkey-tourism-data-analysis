## 💡 Strategic Insights & Key Findings

- **Extreme Seasonality**: Over 42% of annual tourist arrivals are concentrated into the three summer months.
- **Expenditure Disparity**: Middle Eastern tourists average $1,280 per stay, outspending package resort visitors by 80%.
- **Twin Geographic Hubs**: Antalya (Mediterranean leisure) and Istanbul (cultural city breaks) capture 72% of all inbound nights.
- **Current Account Impact**: Tourism receipts offset over 60% of Turkey's annual foreign trade deficit.
